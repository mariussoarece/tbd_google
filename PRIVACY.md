# ToBeDone – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

ToBeDone is a to-do and shopping list app.

- **No data collection.** Everything you enter (tasks, shopping, monthly, grocery and other lists, and your settings)
  is stored only on your device.
- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party services.** The app does not connect to the internet.
- **Notifications.** The app asks for permission to show reminders on the day a task is due.
  Reminders are created and shown locally on your device.
- **Backup.** If you have Android backup turned on, your lists may be included in your device's
  Google backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.
- **Deleting data.** Delete items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data.
